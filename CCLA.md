# Corporate Contributor License Agreement

**HydroCouple Foundation**
Version 1.0

This Corporate Contributor License Agreement ("CCLA") is entered into between the Steward of
the Project (as defined in Section 1) and the organization identified in Schedule B
("Organization").

This CCLA is the corporate counterpart to the Individual Contributor License Agreement at
[CLA.md](./CLA.md). It does not replace the Individual CLA. Individuals must still sign the
Individual CLA in their own right.

---

## 1. Definitions

- **"Project"** means the software repository in which this file is located, together with
  any additional repository listed in Schedule C.
- **"Steward"** means HydroCouple Foundation, Inc., a Kentucky nonprofit corporation, or,
  until that corporation has been formed and this CCLA assigned to it under Section 8, the
  individual holding the Technical Manager role for the Project as defined in
  [CLA.md](./CLA.md).
- **"Designated Employees"** means the individuals listed in Schedule A, as amended under
  Section 7.
- **"Contribution"** means any original work of authorship, including modifications or
  additions to an existing work, submitted by a Designated Employee to the Project, in which
  the Organization owns or claims, or may own or claim, any copyright or patent interest.
- **"Successor Steward"** means any entity that subsequently assumes stewardship of the
  Project, and any successor holder of the Technical Manager role.

The Project is defined by repository. This CCLA does not reach works in other repositories
unless they are listed in Schedule C.

2. Each Designated Employee is authorized to submit Contributions to the Project on the terms

## 7. Schedule Maintenance

The Organization may add or remove Designated Employees by written notice, including email,
to the Steward. Removal is effective prospectively only and does not affect the licenses
granted for Contributions already submitted.

---

## 8. Assignment

This CCLA and all rights granted under it are assignable in whole by the Steward to
HydroCouple Foundation, Inc. upon its formation and thereafter to any Successor Steward,
without further consent of the Organization. The Organization will be notified in writing of
any such assignment.

---

## 9. Term and Survival

This CCLA takes effect on the date of signature and continues until terminated by either
party on thirty (30) days' written notice.

Termination is prospective only. The licenses granted in Sections 2 and 3 with respect to
Contributions submitted before termination are irrevocable and survive termination of this
CCLA, any change in the Organization's policies, and any change in the employment status of
any Designated Employee.

---

This CCLA is governed by the laws of the Commonwealth of Kentucky, without regard to its
conflict of law provisions.

## 11. Entire Agreement

This CCLA, together with its Schedules, is the entire agreement between the parties on its
subject matter. It may be amended only in a writing signed by both parties, except that
Schedule A may be amended under Section 7.
