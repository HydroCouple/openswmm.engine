## Discovered SIGMA conservation gap and next step

With `Ks=0.01 m/s`, a 4 m² cell and no source stage or ET demand, the largest one-firing residual is **0.984802466 m³**. Some laws fail at smaller intervals too. The unchanged parent `fireCell` and `SigmaColumn` hashes confirm this is independent of the new area-budget API. The early all-closure common-stage stress fixture mixed this table movement with atmospheric extraction; the final extraction-site checks isolate ET from recharge, and do not certify those failing dynamics. First-pass logs and a standalone reproduction remain visible.
