# Spatial groundwater transport

This development implementation extends the CPU mesh aquifer. Restricted SSP-RK2 profiles cover groundwater reactions, transported water temperature and porous soil heat with chemistry/age; finite surface/soil heat and chemical/age contacts are also qualified under the restrictions below. It does not apply to conventional subcatchment `[GROUNDWATER]` aquifers. Build from the current source; published packages may lag these interfaces.

## Supported profiles

| Profile | Authoring | Storage and execution |
|---|---|---|
| Bulk groundwater | Default `UNSAT_TRANSPORT BULK` | SAT and mixed UNSAT inventories; existing closures and defaults |
| Layer chemistry | `UNSAT_TRANSPORT LAYERED` | SIGMA on every covered cell; aqueous plus sorbed layer inventories; UNSAT is their derived sum |
| Exterior edges | `[2D_AQUIFER_BOUNDARIES]` and `[GW_BOUNDARY_QUALITY]` | HEAD or FLOW hydraulics; advective composition and independent mass/heat fluxes |
| Aquifer reactions | `[REACTION_SUBSURFACE]` | Explicit SAT/UNSAT/LAYER expressions using the shared reaction integrator |
| Porous soil heat | `THERMAL_MODEL POROUS_EQUILIBRIUM` | Fixed-depth thermal/porewater owner; boundary water, species and physical joules are booked once |

LAYERED chemistry can use `THERMAL_MODEL WATER_ONLY` or `POROUS_EQUILIBRIUM`. With porous heat, SIGMA owns solute/age mass and fixed physical layers own thermal energy; a sparse conservative overlap map connects their independently chosen layer counts. Bulk porous SAT/UNSAT reactions retain their fixed-depth inventory owner. LAYERED requires spatial `MODE MESH`. Coupled surface reconstruction order 2 supports pollutants, MSX, age, groundwater reactions and water-temperature transport with WATER_ONLY and coupled heat disabled in the standalone CPU profile below. A separate porous RK2 profile adds stored soil energy, conduction and chemical/age inventories under constant, authored time-series or held air thermal boundaries, including latent soil evaporation. Finite surface/soil contacts have the additional profile below. Groundwater stores and time advancement use the built-in CPU owner. The optional OpenMP raw-recharge operator below is a separate partial profile; CUDA/HIP/SYCL requests retain CPU groundwater computation. `swmm_2d_get_run_stats` identifies the surface marcher, while `swmm_gw2d_get_execution_status` identifies the groundwater operator.

## Restricted CPU SSP-RK2

A standalone spatial `CLOSED_FORM`, `ENSLAVED` or `SIGMA` aquifer (including mixed cells) can advance with `MOMENTUM_EQUATION FULL_SWE` and `RECONSTRUCTION_ORDER 2` on the CPU. Set `NODE_ENROLMENT ROWS` without exchange rows and `LINK_SEEPAGE NONE`. This profile supports water-only runs or pollutant/MSX/age/water-temperature transport and explicit groundwater reactions, with BULK or SIGMA LAYERED UNSAT inventories. Active surface transport requires matching complete surface/groundwater rows. The WATER_ONLY profile requires no porous/contact/atmospheric heat, subcatchments, network exchange points, runtime surface boundary providers, snow or borrowed common-source/channel owners. Porous heat with optional chemical/age rows has its own restricted profile below. Enabled surface pollutant decay and BULK MSX PIPE kinetics use the same owned symmetric reaction stage. Wells and aquifer HEAD/FLOW edges may have constant or authored piecewise-linear water and passive quality forcing. The marcher uses one global tier; unsupported profiles fail before execution.

Authored well/FLOW-edge knots and sign reversals split the shared surface/groundwater step. Both Euler evaluations use the exact mean prescribed water flow on the same interval, so the convex blend counts each forcing interval once. Earlier accepted subintervals are the starting point for subsequent blends; a later failure restores the complete original call. Gross boundary requested/applied volumes and domain ledgers receive the same accepted blend. HEAD stages integrate pressure-driven flux with their held cell head; no additional aquifer-only water substeps occur inside an Euler evaluation. Shared cuts also include exterior-head clipping levels. Quality-series knots also split the shared stages. Runtime providers remain guarded.

Both stages advance surface and groundwater water together. The accepted convex blend includes SAT/UNSAT water, pending surface returns, wetting-front state and cumulative transfer ledgers. CLOSED_FORM stores blend directly. ENSLAVED cells blend total water `W = theta_s * hg + hu`, invert the existing equilibrium relation on the bounded table interval, and recover `hu` at equilibrium. Averaging table height or moisture separately would violate that relation. Stage intake uses the closure's suction and an instantaneous drying-front derivative; inconsistent initial equilibrium state is refused. A rejected trial or failed advance restores the complete owned numerical state, including momentum, clocks and receipts. Smooth stages evaluate instantaneous intake and drying recovery; reusing their held-interval finite subflows would reduce coupled temporal order. At the singular initial wetting front a finite Green-Ampt startup retains bounded intake, without claiming second-order accuracy across that event.

For ENSLAVED Gardner drainage, an independent scalar RK4 reference refined from 20,000 to 40,000 steps agrees within `1e-13`; four RK2 refinements reduce table-height error from `3.04e-5` to `4.62e-7` at approximately second order. Smooth coupled intake and full-step/two-half-step differences meet the stated second global/third local order on four fine steps; coarser pre-asymptotic measurements are retained. All four soil laws have conserved-water blend, dry/saturated-bound, signed forcing, exact restart and late-failure rollback gates. Mixed CLOSED_FORM/ENSLAVED cells retain their respective canonical storage rules. These results do not claim the same order through singular startup or capillary-fringe switching.

SIGMA stages evaluate gravity, optional capillary flux and ALE grid velocity from their starting state; the bounded table solve settles capacity and donor constraints. Saturated water and each layer's `theta * L / M_LAYERS` inventory receive the same convex blend, then moisture and bulk UNSAT storage are derived. External withdrawals cannot spend proposed recharge before the column accepts it. Strict initial layer bounds and layer/bulk consistency are checked before execution; only machine-roundoff excursions in derived moisture are normalized. An independent Gardner layer/table/surface ODE reference agrees within `1e-12` under 20,000/40,000-step RK4 refinement. Four RK2 refinements reduce rising/falling-table error from `3.90e-6`/`3.08e-6` to `6.03e-8`/`4.79e-8`; full-step/two-half-step differences approach third local order. A separate capillary trajectory retains second temporal order. All four soil laws pass canonical layer-water blends, dry residual starts, collapse/rewet, signed wells/HEAD/FLOW edges, mixed closures, atmospheric ledgers, exact component restart and late-failure rollback checks. Bounds/conservation gates through collapse or donor switching do not establish temporal order at those events. The first-order column path retains its existing ALE update.

Tests measure approximately fourfold error reduction for analytical deep drainage and nonlinear lateral Darcy relaxation, and factors 3.85/3.91 for smooth coupled infiltration under timestep halving. Full-step/two-half-step differences approach third local order on four fine steps; the coarsest measurement is retained as pre-asymptotic evidence. Large injection/extraction and singular startup conserve water and stay admissible. An affine well plus linear deep drainage also converges at second order over four timestep refinements. Constant-head unconfined exchange matches an independent Riccati solution under both inflow and drainage. A clipped/reversing head series is checked against an independently refined scalar RK4 reference: the four finest timestep errors decrease by factors about 3.85, 3.69 and 4.00. All six measurements are retained; the two coarsest have cancellation and are not monotonic. This is an asymptotic result for the named fixture, not a monotonic-error or universal order claim across switching/saturation events. Asynchronous signed wells, bounded pumping, late-interval rollback and exact series-qualified checkpoint continuation have separate gross-water gates.

Passive stage transport reads beginning concentrations and aqueous/sorption capacity. Advective exports share finite beginning donor inventories; incoming packets cannot finance another advective Euler export. Independent imposed mass fluxes retain their separate dissolved-availability limits. SIGMA uses explicit conservative layer moves in these stages; the existing implicit first-order column solve is unchanged. Bulk moving-table transport carries dissolved water and the swept sorbed soil capacity separately. Smooth ENSLAVED stages use the beginning equilibrium tangent, avoiding an endpoint-dependent first-order subflow. Canonical mass, paired pending packets, boundary receipts and cumulative ledgers receive the same convex blend as water; layered UNSAT remains a derived sum. Water-age production uses beginning physical water volume in both domains and has its own cumulative ledger. Surface aging is skipped in the later routing-batch split for this profile.

Independent retarded stirred-aquifer and three-store column solutions test second temporal order. Separate shared surface/column refinement, all-four-soil saturation/pumping/rewet, donor exhaustion, dry retained mass, exact species/age restart and whole-call rollback gates cover the named CPU profiles. Coarse measurements exceeding a fixture's absolute accuracy target are retained; order/accuracy claims use its four qualified finer steps. These are temporal results for first-order spatial species fluxes, without a second-order claim across capacity/donor switching.

Groundwater total-mass decay and explicit SAT/UNSAT/LAYER reactions advance by symmetric half steps around each shared transport interval, including forcing-knot cuts. Decay is itself symmetric around MSX kinetics when another species depends on a decaying precursor; duplicate decay ownership for the same species remains refused. Reactions use actual aqueous/sorption capacities and signed production/loss ledgers. RK5 affine stirred-reactor and retarded conversion refinements exercise second-order splitting; EUL remains first-order, and FORMULA/EQUIL projections or discontinuous laws have no blanket second-order claim. High-accuracy implicit kinetics may retry a work or substep-floor limit through at most eight interval bisections at unchanged tolerances. Exhausted retries fail the whole numerical transaction. Exact reaction component continuation, changed-law refusal and later-reactor failure/retry preserve the entire numerical owner. Surface PIPE kinetics and pollutant decay likewise use symmetric half steps, private species/workspace state and a cumulative signed native loss/production ledger. Surface laws may depend on enabled BULK species/pollutants and DT/TEMP (local transported temperature when enabled, otherwise the configured constant); unavailable hydraulic, groundwater and WALL dependencies are refused. Dry retained mass does not react. An independent coupled Gardner surface/four-layer transport, dispersion and reaction ODE has errors `6.73e-6`, `1.67e-6`, `4.16e-7` and `1.04e-7` over four temporal refinements. Surface conversion, decaying-precursor, stiff, algebraic, age-exclusion, exact restart and later-cell/knot failure gates qualify this named profile. Nonfinite rates at an accepted state are hard failures; retries cannot repair an invalid DT-dependent law by changing its interval. Porous energy and conduction have the additional profile below; finite heat-contact staging remains outstanding.

For signed water temperature, enable `HEAT_TRANSPORT YES`, `TRANSPORT_TEMPERATURE YES` in both domains, surface `HEAT_MODEL TRANSPORT_ONLY` and groundwater `THERMAL_MODEL WATER_ONLY`. Matching temperature rows are required. The canonical quantity is °C·m³; the runtime water heat capacity converts imposed W/m edge forcing into that quantity and joins the checkpoint identity. Cold initial/boundary Celsius values are accepted; chemical and age values remain nonnegative. Beginning-state signed exports and cold infiltration/refunds are never clipped as chemical mass. Temperature does not age or react, and each reactor reads its own local temperature. This adds no grain-energy storage, latent cooling or reaction heat.

Independent stirred-water and moving four-layer advection/dispersion references give second-order temperature errors across zero; the latter falls from `1.27e-5` to `1.95e-7` over four refinements. Cold well/HEAD/FLOW receipts, no-water signed heat flux, sensible ET, age/temperature separation, exact component continuation, mode/capacity refusal and whole-call failure/retry qualify this transport-only profile. Stored soil heat with optional chemical/age rows has the additional profile below; coupled surface/contact heat requires the finite-contact profile below. Surface initial age remains authored in hours; public age values and groundwater source/boundary age use seconds.

Forty public closure/forcing cases cover both 4184 and 4186 J/kg/K. Surface atmospheric exchange remains outstanding for RK2.

## Optional host OpenMP recharge

`BACKEND OMP` (or `OPENSWMM_2D_BACKEND=omp`) may select the Kokkos OpenMP extension for the **raw CLOSED_FORM recharge calculation only**. AUTO retains scalar groundwater recharge until an integrated workload qualifies an automatic selection policy. All four soil-characteristic laws call the same scalar soil function inside a host-parallel loop. Their existing physical-model limitations still apply. SIGMA and ENSLAVED recharge, water budgets/donor caps, pending receipts, transport/dispersion, reactions, heat and clocks remain CPU-owned. This also applies when CLOSED_FORM recharge accompanies bulk reactive or porous thermal owners. It is partial host execution; it does not establish full groundwater acceleration or device execution.

The optional ABI v1 advertises the operator, closure, soil laws, host-owned companions, time integrators and canonical host restart. It owns no layer or species arrays. Missing exports, incompatible capabilities or malformed/nonfinite results preserve scalar CPU computation. No CUDA/HIP/SYCL extension is supplied. Kokkos initializes once per process; the first initialization fixes the actual host thread count.

Query `swmm_gw2d_get_execution_status` with `struct_size=sizeof(SWMM_GwExecutionStatus)`, or Python `solver.surface2d.groundwater.execution_status`, after initialization. Availability and successful synchronous operator calls are separate: an available extension has not necessarily run. Batch/cell counters describe accepted raw evaluations, including values that can require scalar recomputation after a source knot changes their input. They are process-local diagnostics, reset on component restore, and excluded from rejected RK2 transactions. The returned status also names the CPU-owned operations and effective Kokkos threads. Surface backend status alone does not describe this partial profile.

Model-qualified groundwater and standalone RK2 checkpoints distinguish the selected operator profile. Same-profile restart restores canonical host inventories and recomputes transient operator inputs; changing the selected operator is refused before state installation. Historical bulk native hotstarts remain state-only carriers and do not certify exact session continuation. The full-engine/output and RK2 profile restrictions elsewhere in this document remain applicable.

```text
[GW_BOUNDARY_QUALITY]
1 0 TSS CONC 3
2 2 TSS TS GW_TSS
2 2 TSS MASSFLUX 2
```

Use cells and exterior local edges from the actual mesh. File cells are **1-based**; local edges are **0-based**. For triangles and quads, edge `e` joins local vertices `(e+1)%nv` and `(e+2)%nv`. Interior or duplicate hydraulic edges, unmatched quality rows and uncovered aquifer cells are refused.

HEAD is an elevation in project length units. FLOW is **total m³/s across the edge, positive inward**, including for US-unit projects. An unspecified edge is no-flow. HEAD and named FLOW series may reverse. Every enabled groundwater species needs an explicit exterior concentration, including zero, wherever inflow is possible. Extraction uses the interior donor. A concentration and one independent imposed flux may coexist for each species/edge.

CONC/TS uses native concentration, age in seconds, and signed temperature in °C. MASSFLUX is native amount/(s·m of edge); HEATFLUX is W/m. Physical edge length is applied once. Mass flux requires a pollutant or BULK reaction species with a recognized amount basis; age/temperature do not accept MASSFLUX. HEATFLUX requires temperature transport. A zero hydraulic FLOW can carry an independent imposed species/heat flux. A quality row alone never supplies water.

Forcing is integrated chronologically across series knots and sign changes. Outgoing water and nonnegative species sinks are availability-limited. Gross requested/accepted water and advective/imposed species receipts remain separate, including when net water exchange is zero. Boundary water and species enter the lateral net ledger once; they are not disguised as wells.

The new **authored** Python boundary API is `groundwater.set_authored_boundary(...)`, before initialization. Its inward FLOW convention differs from the existing runtime `set_flow_boundary(...)` API's outward-flow argument. Both document their units; overlapping authored/runtime prescriptions are refused.

## Layers and reactions

```text
[2D_AQUIFER_OPTIONS]
CLOSURE SIGMA
M_LAYERS 4

[GW_TRANSPORT_OPTIONS]
UNSAT_TRANSPORT LAYERED

[GW_INITIAL_QUALITY]
* UNSAT TSS 2
CELL 1 LAYER 2 TSS 7

[REACTION_SPECIES]
BULK A MG
BULK B MG

[REACTION_SUBSURFACE]
* SAT A RATE -0.001*A
* SAT B RATE 0.001*A
* UNSAT A RATE -0.001*A
* UNSAT B RATE 0.001*A
```

These are authoring fragments: the model must also enable the corresponding processes and define its mesh/aquifer, species and reaction options. Reaction rates follow `[REACTION_OPTIONS] RATE_UNITS`.

Authored LAYER indices start at **1**. Runtime C/Python layer access and HDF5 layer axes start at **0**, from surface downward. Precedence is GLOBAL < TAG < CELL; within a scope, UNSAT initializes all layers before explicit LAYER values. An invalid/non-SIGMA layer request is refused rather than mapped to a bulk store.

Each layer stores total aqueous plus equilibrium-sorbed amount. Dissolved concentration is `M / (Vwater + Kd × bulk_density × Vbulk)`; authored Kd in L/kg is converted to m³/kg once. Transport consumes only the accepted hydraulic layer transfers, including moving-table redistribution, compression and support. An implicit conservative chain solve handles internal layer transfers and vertical dispersion. Dry retained inventory stays visible. Layer sums exactly define the UNSAT inventory. In LAYERED mode the existing SAT/UNSAT concentration channels use total inventory divided by aqueous-plus-sorption capacity; legacy BULK keeps its historical inventory/water-volume reporting basis. Layered age production is a separately ledgered term; legacy BULK age semantics remain unchanged.

Subsurface expressions may use DT, TEMP, GW_THETA, GW_POROSITY, GW_SE and GW_DEPTH_M, along with supported species, coefficients and intermediate terms. UNSAT laws run independently in each active layer; scoped LAYER laws override them. No PIPE/TANK expression is reused implicitly. Pipe-only variables, reachable WALL species, disabled pollutant dependencies and duplicate first-order decay ownership are refused. Unrelated network WALL species do not disable groundwater BULK species. Aqueous RATE kinetics are scaled to aqueous volume rather than accidentally reacting sorbed inventory at the same rate. Reaction production can appear as negative net loss. Expressions can be edited through the existing C/Python staged reaction-text interface; failed edits preserve the previous system.

In LAYERED mode, node leakage above the table enters the SIGMA layer intersected by the bed base (node invert minus bed thickness). An unsaturated bed cannot drain the disconnected saturated zone upward. Conduit leakage is split along each clipped polyline segment at the actual layer/table intersections, using linearly interpolated endpoint invert elevations and authored bed thickness. Accepted water and producer composition share the same receiving slots. Slots remain fixed until the owner fires; the accepted ALE sweep then moves their inventory conservatively. Compression can pass excess downward into SAT, with saturation excess handled by the existing surface return. Default BULK retains its historical saturated-contact approximation.

Porous SAT/UNSAT reactions use each fixed physical layer's geometric table overlap, aqueous water and sorption capacity. They read the thermal owner's local temperature and phase depth/moisture. Accepted outgoing reservations remain protected from later reactions. All physical-layer reaction updates stage together before one canonical inventory is published and bulk views are refreshed. This adds no reaction heat source; heat-producing chemistry is not implied.

### SIGMA chemistry with porous soil heat

Enable `HEAT_TRANSPORT YES`, `UNSAT_TRANSPORT LAYERED`, `THERMAL_MODEL POROUS_EQUILIBRIUM`, and SIGMA on every active mesh aquifer cell. `M_LAYERS` controls chemistry; `THERMAL_LAYERS` controls the fixed-depth thermal grid. Their counts may differ. Authored LAYER values, including temperature, select SIGMA layers in this profile. Unspecified initial temperatures use the configured thermal initial state.

Each thermal/chemical interval intersection borrows its actual aqueous and grain capacity. Surface/channel contact increments return to the intersected canonical chemical stores; accepted export reservations remain unavailable to later contacts. The fixed-grid view and its receipts are synchronized at the existing accepted coupling boundaries. Rebuilding the view cannot remix, duplicate or discard canonical layer mass. Zero-capacity retained inventories remain with their chemical owner and cannot supply a wet contact.

Thermal energy advances once on the fixed physical grid using the accepted external water/heat receipts and overlap-projected moisture. Water-temperature quantities on SIGMA layers are derived from aqueous overlap with the thermal field; they are not separate soil-energy stores. Reactions read that projected temperature and the actual chemical-layer aqueous/sorption capacity. Porous solid energy is never advected as solute or produced implicitly by reaction expressions. Coarse thermal layers mix temperatures within each physical control volume; refining chemistry alone does not refine the thermal solution.

Groundwater transport state checkpoints include overlap geometry, protected exports and pending contact increments. Incompatible thermal/chemical grids and unpaired mapping receipts fail before changing the target. Standalone thermal hotstart save/apply remains refused by the existing carrier guard. The attached mesh-only engine checkpoint profile below also qualifies calendar and groundwater-output continuation; wider owner combinations retain their separate restrictions.
