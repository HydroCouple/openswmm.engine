(9 tests). `verification_summary.json` records scope and source hashes.

spatial equations with NumPy/SciPy Radau. The final comparison is in

1/16-column workload at 8/32/64 porous cells per column. The earlier
`richards_cost.csv` was interrupted and collected during builds; do not use
it as a controlled whole-model estimate. Neither timing proves a SIMD/GPU
speedup. For equal physical-layer counts, refine cells and check results.

bed rejection and explicit EXCHANGE NO opt-out.
